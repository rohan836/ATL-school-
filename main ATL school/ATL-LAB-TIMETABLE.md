# ATL Lab Weekly Timetable

## Overview and Operating Model

This timetable integrates hands-on Electronics practicals with foundational Artificial Intelligence (AI) lectures within a single, static weekly schedule.

- **Class Structure:** Classes 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9. Each class is split into two batches: **Batch C** (approx. 20 students) and **Batch D** (approx. 20 students).
- **Class 5 and Class 6 (3 sessions per week):**
  - **Day 1:** 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (70–75 min).
  - **Day 2:** 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (70–75 min).
  - **Day 3 (Last Day):** Split session — First 35–40 minutes Electronics Project Wrap-up & Testing; **Last 35 minutes dedicated AI Lecture**.
- **Class 7, Class 8, and Class 9 (2 sessions per week):**
  - **Day 1:** 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (65–70 min).
  - **Day 2 (Last Day):** Split session — First 35 minutes Electronics Practical & Testing; **Last 30–35 minutes dedicated AI Lecture**.
- **Overall Weekly Subject Balance:**
  - Classes 5 and 6: 84% Electronics / 16% AI.
  - Classes 7, 8, and 9: 74–75% Electronics / 25–26% AI.

## Weekly Progression Flow

- **Classes 5 and 6 (3 Days):**
  - **Day 1:** Circuit breadboarding, component study, and baseline assembly.
  - **Day 2:** Wiring sensors, testing functionality, and troubleshooting errors.
  - **Day 3 (First Half - 35 to 40 min):** Final testing, demonstration, and filling out the Student Assignment Sheet.
  - **Day 3 (Second Half - 35 min):** Dedicated AI lecture on interactive smart systems and pattern recognition.

- **Classes 7, 8, and 9 (2 Days):**
  - **Day 1:** System architecture, breadboarding, and primary coding/circuit build.
  - **Day 2 (First Half - 35 min):** Calibration, live testing, and documentation on the Student Assignment Sheet.
  - **Day 2 (Second Half - 30 to 35 min):** Dedicated AI lecture connecting hardware sensors to machine learning logic.
