## Asset Care, Safety & Annual Verification Guidelines

1. **Daily Check-Out & Check-In**: Every tool removed from the wall-mounted pegboard or workbench station must be wiped clean and returned to its designated hook or cradle at the end of the class.
2. **Sharps & Cutting Tools**: Hacksaw frames, needle files, and drill bits must be inspected for wear and stored in their protective cases.
3. **Power Equipment Supervision**: The electric drill machine, drill press workstation, rotary multitool, 1800W heat gun, and 3D printer must only be operated under teacher/lab assistant supervision with mandatory safety goggles.
4. **Annual Stock Audit**: Use this master list to perform the yearly fixed-asset stock audit for Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) reporting.
