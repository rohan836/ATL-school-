# Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL) — Unified Electronics Curriculum (Classes 5 to 9)

> **Curriculum Model**: Single Progressive Master Curriculum for Classes 5th to 9th Standard  
> **Lab Context**: Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL) — 90 Students Capacity (Batches of 30, Teams of 2–3)  
> **Foundational Pedagogy**: 
> 1. *Electronics for Kids: Play with Simple Circuits and Experiment with Electricity* (Oyvind Nydal Dahl) — Intuitive hands-on play, visual circuits, beginner confidence.
> 2. *The Secret Life of Circuits: An Illustrated Guide to Electronic Circuit Design* (Michal Zalewski) — Deep engineering intuition, component physics, power regulation, signal conditioning, and systematic bench debugging.
> 3. *ATL Equipment List (90 Students)* — Direct integration with all Package 1, 2, 3, and 4 lab inventory (Arduinos, sensors, discrete components, multimeters, soldering stations, oscilloscope).

---

## 1. Pedagogical Architecture & Grade Progression

```
[CLASS 5: THE SPARK] ───► Tangible electricity, lemon cells, electromagnetism, alligator clips, breadboard discovery.
         │
[CLASS 6: THE CONTROLLER] ──► DMM multimeter mastery, Ohm's law, resistor color codes, capacitors & fading circuits.
         │
[CLASS 7: THE SWITCH] ──► Transistors (BJT) as switches/amplifiers, relays, LDR light alarms, 555 Timer oscillators.
         │
[CLASS 8: THE SENSOR & BENCH] ─► Op-Amps (LM358 comparators), 7805 voltage regulators, sensors, soldering license.
         │
[CLASS 9: THE SMART SYSTEM] ──► Microcontrollers (Arduino Uno/Nano/NodeMCU), ADC, PWM, LCDs, IoT, Capstone Engineering.
```

- **Class 5 (Foundations & Physical Discovery)**: No math barrier. Experiential learning, safety, conductors/insulators, breadboard layout, simple LED circuits.
- **Class 6 (Quantification & Analog Control)**: Introducing numbers, measurements using multimeters (volts, ohms, amps), resistance color bands, capacitor charge/discharge curves, diodes and LED voltage drops.
- **Class 7 (Active Semiconductors & Timing)**: Moving from passive components to active switching. Transistors (BC547/BC557), relays for load isolation, 555 Timer IC for musical tones and flashing lights.
- **Class 8 (Sensor Interfacing, Precision & Workshop Craft)**: Analog sensor conditioning using LM358 op-amp comparators, linear power regulation (7805/7812), hands-on bench soldering on Zero-PCB, desoldering, and circuit troubleshooting.
- **Class 9 (Intelligent Systems & ATL Capstone)**: Merging hardware electronics with Arduino Uno/ESP8266 microcontrollers, sensor-actuator loops, I2C displays, debugging with the lab oscilloscope, and building multi-stage Capstone innovation projects.

## 3. Approved Capstone Innovation Project Portfolio (Class 9 / Advanced)

Students work in teams of 2–3 utilizing the full breadth of ATL equipment (Package 1–4):

1. **Smart Agriculture Irrigation System**:
   - *Hardware*: Soil Moisture Sensor (FC-28) + LM358 / Arduino + 12V DC Submersible Water Pump + 5V Relay Module + 16×2 I2C LCD.
   - *Function*: Automatically monitors soil saturation; activates water pump when moisture falls below calibrated threshold; displays real-time moisture % on LCD.

2. **Automated Industrial Air Quality & Gas Leakage Sentinel**:
   - *Hardware*: MQ-2 / MQ-135 Gas Sensor + Active Buzzer + RGB Alert LED + 7805 Regulated Power Supply + 16×2 LCD + 12V Exhaust Fan.
   - *Function*: Detects toxic smoke/LPG; triggers loud audible buzzer, flashes red alert, activates exhaust motor, and transmits status.

3. **Touchless Robotic Hand & Bio-Medical Pulse Monitor**:
   - *Hardware*: Flex Sensors + Pulse-Rate Heart Sensor + Micro Servos (SG90) + Mechanical Construction Kit / 3D Printed Fingers.
   - *Function*: Replicates human finger flexion using servo mechanical linkages; displays live BPM pulse rate on OLED/LCD.

4. **Earthquake & Structural Vibration Warning System**:
   - *Hardware*: Triple-Axis Accelerometer / Piezoelectric plate sensor + LM358 Comparator + 555 Timer Latching Siren + High-power LED beacon.
   - *Function*: Senses seismic vibrations; triggers an immediate latching emergency alarm that stays active until manually reset.

5. **Solar-Powered Clean 5V/12V ATL Lab Bench Power Supply**:
   - *Hardware*: 7805 & 7812 Linear Regulators + Electrolytic Capacitors + Diode Bridge + DC Voltmeter / DMM readout + Zero-PCB soldered enclosure with heat sink.
   - *Function*: Delivers dual regulated outputs with thermal overload protection and LED status indicators for bench prototyping.

## 5. Lab Management for 90 Students (Organized Batches)

To ensure high-quality hands-on engagement with 90 enrolled students:
1. **Batch Rotation**:
   - Total Cohort: 90 Students.
   - 3 Class Batches of 30 students each (e.g., Batch A, B, C or Class 5/6, Class 7, Class 8/9).
   - In-Lab Teams: 10 workbench stations per batch (3 students per station).
2. **Station Component Kits**:
   - Each bench is permanently allocated 1 Breadboard (800-pin), 1 DMM, 1 Wire Stripper, and a standardized component caddy (Resistors, Capacitors, LEDs, Transistors, Jumpers).
   - Specialized tools (Soldering stations, 3D printer, Oscilloscope) are accessed via dedicated supervised technician zones with mandatory safety goggles.
3. **Traceability & Notebooks**:
   - Every student maintains an **ATL Electronics Tinkering Logbook** where they draw schematics, record measured vs calculated multimeter values, and note debugging observations before teacher sign-off.
