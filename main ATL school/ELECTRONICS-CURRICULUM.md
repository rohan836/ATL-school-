# Basic & Applied Electronics Curriculum — ATL Lab

> **Scope**: Pure Electronics Only (No Robotics, No Microcontrollers)  
> **Target Audience**: Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL) Students (Grades 6–10 / Beginners to Intermediate)  
> **Approach**: Hands-on breadboard prototyping, multimeter measurement, component understanding, and analog circuit design.

## 2. Capstone Analog Electronics Projects List (Pure Electronics)

Students select and assemble any of these hardware projects on breadboard or zero PCB (zero microcontroller / zero coding required):

1. **Automatic Twilight/Night Lamp**: LDR + BC547 Transistor + Relay switching an external light.
2. **Soil Moisture / Water Level Indicator**: 3-level water detector using NPN transistors and colored LEDs (Green = Full, Yellow = Half, Red = Empty).
3. **Fire / Overheating Alarm**: NTC Thermistor + LM358 Comparator + Piezo Buzzer with potentiometer sensitivity calibration.
4. **555 Sound Generator / Electronic Metronome**: NE555 in astable mode driving an 8-ohm speaker through a coupling capacitor.
5. **Adjustable Regulated Bench Power Supply**: LM317 adjustable linear voltage regulator with smoothing capacitors and voltmeter display.
6. **Optical Intrusion / Tripwire Alarm**: Laser pointer + LDR + Latching transistor or SCR circuit with audible alarm.
