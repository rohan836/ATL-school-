# ATL Teaching & Lab Proposal for Principal

> **Document**: [ATL-TEACHING-AND-LAB-OPERATIONAL-PROPOSAL.md](ATL-TEACHING-AND-LAB-OPERATIONAL-PROPOSAL.md)  
> **Prepared by**: ATL Lab Teacher / Electronics & Robotics Instructor  
> **Purpose**: Simple, practical proposal for the Principal explaining how the teacher conducts ATL classes, how 5-student groups work, the grade-wise curriculum, a 4-week Line-Following Car example, and how it benefits students and the school.  

### Core Highlights:
1. **Class Organization**: 40 students per batch $\rightarrow$ 8 working teams of 5 students each.
2. **5 Student Roles**: Mechanical, Circuit Wiring, Programmer, Sensor Specialist, Tester/Logbook.
3. **Grade Progression (5 to 9)**: From basic electric motors in Class 5 up to IoT/Drones in Class 9.
4. **Project Example**: 4-week step-by-step Line-Following Robot Car breakdown.
5. **Clear Benefits**: Practical hands-on skills for students; budget efficiency and school prestige for the institution.
