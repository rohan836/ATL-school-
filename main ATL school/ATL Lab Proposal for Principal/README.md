> **Target Audience**: School Principal & Administrative Leadership  
> **Prepared by**: ATL Lab In-Charge & Electronics / Robotics Instructor  
> **Academic Scope**: Classes 5 to 9 (10 Batches, 400 Total Students)

## Proposal Document
- 📄 **[ATL-TEACHING-AND-LAB-OPERATIONAL-PROPOSAL.md](ATL-TEACHING-AND-LAB-OPERATIONAL-PROPOSAL.md)**:  
  Formal, executive-level institutional proposal written from teacher to principal detailing:
  1. **Demographics**: 400 students across Classes 5 to 9 (Batches C & D per grade, 40 students per batch).
  2. **Classroom Pedagogy**: 8 groups per session (5 students per group with clear defined roles).
  3. **Curriculum Progression**: Age-appropriate engineering progression from Class 5 (basic circuits) through Class 9 (IoT and Drones).
  4. **Case Study**: Full 4-week lesson plan and component math for the **Autonomous Line-Following Robot Car** project.
  5. **Operational Accountability**: The 80-bin "Class Project Box" system to prevent component loss.
  6. **Safety Infrastructure**: Supervised perimeter soldering bay (6–8 stations) and 100% safety goggle compliance.
  7. **Financial Efficiency**: Clear distinction between one-time Permanent Capital Assets and recurring Project Consumables.
  8. **Approval Block**: Official signature and endorsement section.
