ATL Lab Weekly Timetable

Overview and Operating Model

This timetable is based on the original school master timetable, seamlessly integrating hands-on Electronics practicals with foundational Artificial Intelligence (AI) lectures within a single, static weekly schedule.

  • Class Structure: Classes 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9. Each class is split into two batches: Batch C (approx. 20 students) and Batch D (approx. 20 students).
  • Class 5 (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday — 3 consecutive days):
    • Monday (Day 1): 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (70–75 min).
    • Tuesday (Day 2): 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (70–75 min).
    • Wednesday (Day 3 - Final Day): Split session — First 35–40 minutes Electronics Project Wrap-up & Testing; Last 35 minutes dedicated AI Lecture.
  • Class 6 (Thursday, Friday, Saturday — 3 consecutive days):
    • Thursday (Day 1): 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (70–75 min).
    • Friday (Day 2): 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (70–75 min).
    • Saturday (Day 3 - Final Day): Split session — First 35–40 minutes Electronics Project Wrap-up & Testing; Last 35 minutes dedicated AI Lecture.
  • Class 7 (Monday, Tuesday — 2 consecutive days):
    • Monday (Day 1): 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (65–70 min).
    • Tuesday (Day 2 - Final Day): Split session — First 35 minutes Electronics Practical & Testing; Last 30–35 minutes dedicated AI Lecture.
  • Class 8 (Wednesday, Thursday — 2 consecutive days):
    • Wednesday (Day 1): 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (65–70 min).
    • Thursday (Day 2 - Final Day): Split session — First 35 minutes Electronics Practical & Testing; Last 30–35 minutes dedicated AI Lecture.
  • Class 9 (Friday, Saturday — 2 consecutive days):
    • Friday (Day 1): 100% Hands-on Electronics Practical (65–70 min).
    • Saturday (Day 2 - Final Day): Split session — First 30–35 minutes Electronics Practical & Testing; Last 30 minutes dedicated AI Lecture.
