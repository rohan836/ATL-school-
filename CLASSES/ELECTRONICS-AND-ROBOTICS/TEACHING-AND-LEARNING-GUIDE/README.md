This guide explains how to teach electronics and robotics through practical work, what students should learn from Classes 5–9, how to use projects, how to ask useful questions, and how to assess learning.

## Core principle

Students should learn electronics by **building, testing, explaining, and improving**. Teach a concept when students need it for a build. Keep theory short and connected to a real circuit or project.

## Course progression

Students progress from simple circuits to systems that sense, decide, and act:

1. Electricity and basic circuits
2. Components and simple control
3. Measurement and troubleshooting
4. Sensors and microcontrollers
5. Robotics and automation
6. Integrated engineering projects

## Planning note

The class-wise roadmaps and project ideas in this guide are a proposed teaching plan. Confirm lesson counts, available components, and the exact project hardware against the school's timetable and ATL inventory before preparing detailed lesson plans.
