# ATL Lab Teaching Timetable

## Purpose

This timetable schedules 60-minute electronics, robotics, and project-based practical lessons in the Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL) for Classes 5–9. Each class has two batches: C and D.

The timetable uses the school's confirmed Monday–Friday and Saturday bell schedules. Each ATL session is exactly 60 minutes and avoids the school's scheduled breaks. C and D for the same class are taught in the first two available slots for that class, followed by the next class's C and D batches. Sessions on consecutive days continue the same project.

## Fixed constraints

- Classes: 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9.
- Batches: C and D for each class (10 batches).
- Working days: Monday to Saturday (6 days).
- Four 60-minute ATL sessions per day.
- Weekly capacity: 24 sessions × 60 minutes = 1,440 minutes = 24 teaching hours.
- C and D for the same class receive the same practical in sequential slots on the same day.
- Classes 5, 6, and 7 have 2 sessions per batch each week.
- Classes 8 and 9 have 3 sessions per batch each week.
- The school's break times are not included in the 60-minute lesson durations.

## Project continuity across sessions

Continue the same project across each class's scheduled days rather than starting a new practical every session.

- **Class 5 — Monday and Tuesday:** introduce the concept and plan the build on Monday; build, test, and record results on Tuesday.
- **Class 6 — Wednesday and Thursday:** introduce the concept and plan the build on Wednesday; build, test, and record results on Thursday.
- **Class 7 — Friday and Saturday:** introduce the concept and plan the build on Friday; build, test, and record results on Saturday.
- **Class 8 — Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday:** understand the concept and plan on Monday; build and test on Tuesday; improve and demonstrate on Wednesday.
- **Class 9 — Thursday, Friday, and Saturday:** understand the concept and plan on Thursday; build and test on Friday; improve and demonstrate on Saturday.

These are suggested stages. Adapt them to the practical, student progress, and component availability.

## Repeatable 60-minute lesson workflow

1. **0–5 min:** Introduce the challenge and learning goal.
2. **5–15 min:** Explain the concept and demonstrate safe component use.
3. **15–45 min:** Students build or continue the practical.
4. **45–55 min:** Test, troubleshoot, and explain the result.
5. **55–60 min:** Record progress and the next step; reset components where possible.

Teach C and D using the same lesson plan and practical. Keep both batches at a similar project stage.

## Capacity check

- 6 days × 4 sessions per day = 24 sessions per week.
- 24 sessions × 60 minutes = 1,440 minutes = 24 teaching hours per week.
- Classes 5, 6, and 7: 2 sessions per batch × 2 batches = 4 sessions per class per week.
- Classes 8 and 9: 3 sessions per batch × 2 batches = 6 sessions per class per week.
- Total: 3 classes × 4 sessions + 2 classes × 6 sessions = 24 sessions per week.
