# Review Knowledge

Use this workflow when the owner asks for an audit, consistency check, cleanup proposal, or database review.

## Procedure
1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT.md`, and `INDEX.md`.
2. Define the review scope from the owner's request. Inspect only relevant records unless a broader audit was explicitly requested.
3. Check for issues such as:
   - duplicate or conflicting sources;
   - unclear status, date, origin, or context;
   - broken links or stale index entries;
   - facts mixed with plans, ideas, or completed outcomes;
   - records that are difficult to find or understand later;
   - sensitive information that should not be in a public repository.
4. Report findings with specific paths and evidence. Distinguish confirmed issues from suggestions.
5. Propose changes before carrying out additions, deletions, moves, bulk edits, or structural changes unless the owner has explicitly authorized those exact actions.
6. After approved edits, recheck affected records and links.

## Review principles
- Do not rewrite content merely to make it look uniform.
- Keep useful history and provenance.
- Prefer the smallest change that resolves a real retrieval or reliability problem.
- Do not assume that an empty or missing category is a problem; the database should grow from actual needs.
