# Find Knowledge

Use this workflow when the owner asks to recall, locate, compare, or explain information that may already be in the repository.

## Procedure
1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT.md`, and use `INDEX.md` to identify likely sources.
2. Search for the subject, alternate terms, and relevant dates or statuses. Do not rely on the index alone.
3. Read the source record(s) that support the answer. For comparisons, inspect the relevant records on both sides.
4. Separate what the repository explicitly states from conclusions, missing details, and assumptions.
5. Give a concise answer with source paths or links. Include dates and statuses when they materially affect interpretation.
6. If no record supports the answer, say that the database does not currently contain the information. Do not invent a remembered fact.

## Retrieval discipline
- Prefer the authoritative source over summaries or duplicated references.
- If records conflict, describe the conflict and identify each source; do not silently choose one.
- When asked for “latest” or “current,” check dates and whether the record is approved or still valid.
