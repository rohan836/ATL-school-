# AI Teaching List — Units 1–20

Content list only: what to teach, in order. No dates and no week numbers. For the dated version, see [AI-8-WEEK-PROGRAM.md](CLASSES/AI-8-WEEK-PROGRAM.md) and the per-class `8-WEEK-PLAN.md` files.

**Status:** plan, not a record of teaching completed.

## Core units (all classes)

| # | Teach | Practical |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What AI is and is not; rule-based vs learned behaviour | Sort cards twice: once by a written rule, once by example |
| 2 | Data → pattern → prediction | Label the data, pattern, and prediction in three real AI examples |
| 3 | Features and labels | Describe ten objects by measurable features: colour, size, sound |
| 4 | Classification vs generation | Compare three tools and decide which task each performs |
| 5 | Building a dataset: categories, examples, variety | Collect three categories × fifteen examples |
| 6 | Training a classifier | Train in Teachable Machine, or run a recorded human classifier |
| 7 | Testing and measuring accuracy | Test on new examples; write accuracy as a fraction |
| 8 | Data quality, balance, bias | Retrain with better data; compare before and after |
| 9 | Train vs test data; why models flatter themselves | Split a dataset; test only on unseen examples |
| 10 | Model card: data, accuracy, failures, limits | Write a one-page model card |
| 11 | Sensors as machine senses | Read LDR, ultrasonic, or DHT11 values on Arduino |
| 12 | Decision rules and thresholds | Make a threshold switch an LED or buzzer |
| 13 | Actuators: making a decision act | Drive a servo, relay, or pump safely |
| 14 | Connecting a model to hardware | Model or rule → decision → physical output |
| 15 | Capstone: problem, success criteria, build, debug | Team build with a dated log |
| 16 | Testing, documenting, demonstrating | Test against criteria, demonstrate, write a reflection |

## Rules that apply to every unit

- No face recognition, no photographs of students, no student data uploaded anywhere.
- Gas sensors are teaching components, not life-safety alarms.
- Never drive a servo, motor, pump, or relay coil directly from a GPIO pin.
- An idea is not a result: every claim needs a measured accuracy or a recorded test.
- Builds powered from mains are out of scope.
