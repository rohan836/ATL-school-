# Class 7 AI & Python Curriculum Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-ai-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## Term 1: The Connected Foundations (Contents 1–6)
1. **What Is AI?** *(Fixed Rules vs. Learning from Examples)* — **Concept:** Rule code vs. pattern-learning code | **Project:** *Guess-the-Animal Rule vs. AI* (Jupyter)
2. **AI Use Cases** *(How AI Solves Real-World Problems)* — **Concept:** Recommendation & smart assistant apps | **Project:** *Movie & Song Matchmaker* (Jupyter)
3. **Computational Thinking** *(Decomposition, Patterns & Step-by-Step Logic)* — **Concept:** Logic steps & smart searching | **Project:** *20-Questions Number Detective* (Jupyter)
4. **How AI Works** *(Data + Training/Testing + CPU vs. GPU Speed)* — **Concept:** Train/test split + why GPUs run fast math | **Project:** *CPU vs. Fast-Loop Speed Race* (Jupyter)
5. **Programming Basics (Python)** *(ALL taught in every class: `print`, `input`, variables, `if/else`, `loops`)* — **Concept:** Full basics + reusable functions (`def`) | **Project:** *Battle-Math Arena Quiz Game* (Jupyter)
6. **Essential Data Structures** *(Lists & Dictionaries—how Python organizes AI data)* — **Concept:** Lists, dictionaries & `pandas` tables | **Project:** *Class Cricket Stats & Bar Chart* (Jupyter)

## Term 2: Building Real AI Systems (Contents 7–12)
7. **Machine Learning** *(Training a computer to predict & classify from data)* — **Concept:** Predicting numbers & classes (`scikit-learn`) | **Project:** *Study Hours vs. Exam Score Predictor* (Jupyter)
8. **Computer Vision** *(How computers see pixels, faces, hands & objects)* — **Concept:** Real-time hand & pose tracking (`mediapipe`) | **Project:** *Webcam Finger-Count Math Game* (VS Code)
9. **NLP & Chatbots** *(How computers clean, understand & reply to human text)* — **Concept:** Sentiment analysis (`textblob` / `sklearn`) | **Project:** *Movie Review Happy/Sad Meter* (Jupyter)
10. **Deep Learning** *(How multi-layer neural networks spot complex patterns)* — **Concept:** How neural layers recognize sketches | **Project:** *Quick-Draw Sketch Guesser Lab* (Colab)
11. **Generative AI & Prompting** *(How modern LLMs predict tokens & follow clear prompts)* — **Concept:** Controlling tone, steps & structured output | **Project:** *Interactive History Character Bot* (Browser/Jupyter)
12. **AI Safety & Ethics** *(Testing hallucinations, dataset bias & digital privacy)* — **Concept:** Spotting deepfakes & hiding private info | **Project:** *Personal Info Auto-Hider Script* (Jupyter)
