# Class 8 AI & Python Curriculum Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-ai-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## Term 1: The Connected Foundations (Contents 1–6)
1. **What Is AI?** *(Fixed Rules vs. Learning from Examples)* — **Concept:** Hardcoded logic vs. ML decision boundaries | **Project:** *Spam Filter: Rules vs. Learning* (Jupyter)
2. **AI Use Cases** *(How AI Solves Real-World Problems)* — **Concept:** Vision & safety AI in smart cities | **Project:** *Smart Traffic & Crowd Counter* (Jupyter)
3. **Computational Thinking** *(Decomposition, Patterns & Step-by-Step Logic)* — **Concept:** Breaking big apps into clean functions | **Project:** *Secret Password Cracker Game* (VS Code)
4. **How AI Works** *(Data + Training/Testing + CPU vs. GPU Speed)* — **Concept:** Features, labels, accuracy & GPU basics | **Project:** *Overfitting Trap Experiment* (Jupyter)
5. **Programming Basics (Python)** *(ALL taught in every class: `print`, `input`, variables, `if/else`, `loops`)* — **Concept:** Full basics + clean modular scripts | **Project:** *Escape-Room Text Adventure Game* (VS Code)
6. **Essential Data Structures** *(Lists & Dictionaries—how Python organizes AI data)* — **Concept:** Dictionaries, JSON & `numpy` number grids | **Project:** *Pixel-Art Matrix Painter* (Jupyter)

## Term 2: Building Real AI Systems (Contents 7–12)
7. **Machine Learning** *(Training a computer to predict & classify from data)* — **Concept:** Comparing classifiers & checking accuracy | **Project:** *Fake vs. Real Note / Fruit Classifier* (Jupyter)
8. **Computer Vision** *(How computers see pixels, faces, hands & objects)* — **Concept:** Pre-trained object detection (`YOLO Nano`) | **Project:** *5-Line Classroom Object Spotter* (VS Code)
9. **NLP & Chatbots** *(How computers clean, understand & reply to human text)* — **Concept:** Text similarity & smart search (`TF-IDF`) | **Project:** *Instant School Notes Search Engine* (Jupyter)
10. **Deep Learning** *(How multi-layer neural networks spot complex patterns)* — **Concept:** Training a 2-layer neural net (`PyTorch`) | **Project:** *PyTorch Pattern Classifier* (Colab)
11. **Generative AI & Prompting** *(How modern LLMs predict tokens & follow clear prompts)* — **Concept:** Grounding AI with your own chapter text | **Project:** *Textbook Chapter Q&A Helper* (Colab)
12. **AI Safety & Ethics** *(Testing hallucinations, dataset bias & digital privacy)* — **Concept:** Red-teaming chatbots for tricky inputs | **Project:** *Break-the-Bot Safety Challenge* (Jupyter)
