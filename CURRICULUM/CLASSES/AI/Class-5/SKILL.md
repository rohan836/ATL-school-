# Class 5 AI & Python Curriculum Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-ai-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## Term 1: The Connected Foundations (Contents 1–6)
1. **What Is AI?** *(Fixed Rules vs. Learning from Examples)* — **Concept:** Rules vs. Learning via visual games | **Project:** *AI or Not? Mystery Box Game* (Browser)
2. **AI Use Cases** *(How AI Solves Real-World Problems)* — **Concept:** AI in games, phones & school life | **Project:** *Smart School Helper Simulator* (Browser)
3. **Computational Thinking** *(Decomposition, Patterns & Step-by-Step Logic)* — **Concept:** Breaking puzzles into small steps | **Project:** *Emoji Secret Code Breaker* (Jupyter)
4. **How AI Works** *(Data + Training/Testing + CPU vs. GPU Speed)* — **Concept:** Data fuel + 1-worker CPU vs. 100-worker GPU | **Project:** *Clean vs. Messy Data Toy Sorter* (Teachable Machine)
5. **Programming Basics (Python)** *(ALL taught in every class: `print`, `input`, variables, `if/else`, `loops`)* — **Concept:** Full basics using fun guided templates | **Project:** *Magic 8-Ball Fortune Teller Game* (Jupyter)
6. **Essential Data Structures** *(Lists & Dictionaries—how Python organizes AI data)* — **Concept:** Lists & key-value pairs with simple items | **Project:** *Random Superhero Team Maker* (Jupyter)

## Term 2: Building Real AI Systems (Contents 7–12)
7. **Machine Learning** *(Training a computer to predict & classify from data)* — **Concept:** Teaching by showing examples & testing | **Project:** *Rock-Paper-Scissors Webcam AI* (Teachable Machine)
8. **Computer Vision** *(How computers see pixels, faces, hands & objects)* — **Concept:** Pixels, RGB colors & visual detection | **Project:** *RGB Color Mixer & Emoji Detector* (Jupyter)
9. **NLP & Chatbots** *(How computers clean, understand & reply to human text)* — **Concept:** Keyword rules & smart text matching | **Project:** *Funny School Helpdesk Chatbot* (Jupyter)
10. **Deep Learning** *(How multi-layer neural networks spot complex patterns)* — **Concept:** Clue-layers & brain-neuron intuition | **Project:** *Draw-a-Digit Neural Net Game* (Browser/Jupyter)
11. **Generative AI & Prompting** *(How modern LLMs predict tokens & follow clear prompts)* — **Concept:** Next-word guessing + clear prompt rules | **Project:** *AI Riddle & Comic Story Co-Writer* (Browser)
12. **AI Safety & Ethics** *(Testing hallucinations, dataset bias & digital privacy)* — **Concept:** Catching confident wrong answers & privacy | **Project:** *Trick-Riddle AI Lie Detector* (Browser)
