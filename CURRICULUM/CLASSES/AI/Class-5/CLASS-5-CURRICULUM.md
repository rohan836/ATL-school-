# Class 5 AI & Python Curriculum

## Term 1: The Connected Foundations (Contents 1–6)

| # | Content & Core Concept *(Taught in ALL Grades 5–9)* | Class 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **What Is AI?**<br>*(Fixed rules vs. learning from examples)* | **Project:** *AI or Not? Mystery Box* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Sort familiar examples into AI and ordinary automation.<br>**Learn:** Recognize fixed rules, learned patterns and AI limitations. |
| **2** | **AI in Everyday Life**<br>*(Recognition, recommendations, predictions and safe use)* | **Project:** *Smart School Game* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Match school problems to useful AI tools.<br>**Learn:** Recognize AI uses, mistakes and privacy risks. |
| **3** | **Computational Thinking**<br>*(Patterns, logical decisions and step-by-step problem solving)* | **Project:** *Robot Direction Puzzle* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Guide a robot using a sequence of arrows.<br>**Learn:** Practice sequencing, planning and fixing mistakes. |
| **4** | **Python Programming Basics**<br>*(Output, input, variables, conditions and loops at suitable depth)* | **Project:** *Magic Quiz* (Python (guided))<br>**Why:** Change questions and answers in a short starter program.<br>**Learn:** Use print, input, variables and simple if/else; explore a guided loop. |
| **5** | **Understanding Data**<br>*(Collect, organize, label and clean information)* | **Project:** *Superhero Team Maker* (Python (guided))<br>**Why:** Store and choose heroes from a simple list.<br>**Learn:** Understand lists, items and selecting data. |
| **6** | **How AI Learns**<br>*(Training examples, testing, predictions and improvement)* | **Project:** *Pencil vs. Eraser AI* (Browser (Teachable Machine or prepared classifier))<br>**Why:** Train with object examples and test unseen pictures.<br>**Learn:** Understand labels, training, testing and mistakes. |

## Term 2: Building Real AI Systems (Contents 7–12)

| # | Content & Core Concept *(Taught in ALL Grades 5–9)* | Class 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **7** | **Machine Learning**<br>*(Training a computer to predict & classify from data)* | **Concept:** Teaching by showing examples & testing<br>**Project:** *Rock-Paper-Scissors Webcam AI* (Teachable Machine) |
| **8** | **Computer Vision**<br>*(How computers see pixels, faces, hands & objects)* | **Concept:** Pixels, RGB colors & visual detection<br>**Project:** *RGB Color Mixer & Emoji Detector* (Jupyter) |
| **9** | **NLP & Chatbots**<br>*(How computers clean, understand & reply to human text)* | **Concept:** Keyword rules & smart text matching<br>**Project:** *Funny School Helpdesk Chatbot* (Jupyter) |
| **10** | **Deep Learning**<br>*(How multi-layer neural networks spot complex patterns)* | **Concept:** Clue-layers & brain-neuron intuition<br>**Project:** *Draw-a-Digit Neural Net Game* (Browser/Jupyter) |
| **11** | **Generative AI & Prompting**<br>*(How modern LLMs predict tokens & follow clear prompts)* | **Concept:** Next-word guessing + clear prompt rules<br>**Project:** *AI Riddle & Comic Story Co-Writer* (Browser) |
| **12** | **AI Safety & Ethics**<br>*(Testing hallucinations, dataset bias & digital privacy)* | **Concept:** Catching confident wrong answers & privacy<br>**Project:** *Trick-Riddle AI Lie Detector* (Browser) |
