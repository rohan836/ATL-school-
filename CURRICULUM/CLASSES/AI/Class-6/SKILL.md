# Class 6 AI & Python Curriculum Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-ai-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## Term 1: The Connected Foundations (Contents 1–6)
1. **What Is AI?** *(Fixed Rules vs. Learning from Examples)* — **Concept:** Rules vs. Learning + Narrow vs. Smart AI | **Project:** *Beat the Rule-Bot Game* (Jupyter)
2. **AI Use Cases** *(How AI Solves Real-World Problems)* — **Concept:** AI in farming, health & traffic | **Project:** *Crop & Rain Helper Game* (Teachable Machine)
3. **Computational Thinking** *(Decomposition, Patterns & Step-by-Step Logic)* — **Concept:** Flowcharts, patterns & step logic | **Project:** *Treasure Maze Step-Planner* (Jupyter)
4. **How AI Works** *(Data + Training/Testing + CPU vs. GPU Speed)* — **Concept:** Training vs. testing + parallel GPU race | **Project:** *Fruit Ripeness Tester + Speed Race* (Teachable Machine)
5. **Programming Basics (Python)** *(ALL taught in every class: `print`, `input`, variables, `if/else`, `loops`)* — **Concept:** Full basics building interactive mini-games | **Project:** *Rock-Paper-Scissors vs. Computer* (Jupyter)
6. **Essential Data Structures** *(Lists & Dictionaries—how Python organizes AI data)* — **Concept:** Lists & dictionaries for game inventories | **Project:** *Pokemon / Cricket Card Lookup* (Jupyter)

## Term 2: Building Real AI Systems (Contents 7–12)
7. **Machine Learning** *(Training a computer to predict & classify from data)* — **Concept:** Training multi-class sound/image models | **Project:** *Clap-Snap-Whistle Sound Game* (Teachable Machine)
8. **Computer Vision** *(How computers see pixels, faces, hands & objects)* — **Concept:** Image filters & box detection (`opencv`) | **Project:** *Funny Face-Box & Filter Booth* (Jupyter)
9. **NLP & Chatbots** *(How computers clean, understand & reply to human text)* — **Concept:** Word counting, text cleanup & mood words | **Project:** *Story Word-Cloud & Mood Detector* (Jupyter)
10. **Deep Learning** *(How multi-layer neural networks spot complex patterns)* — **Concept:** Weights, thresholds & layered guessing | **Project:** *Pass/Fail Smart Neuron Simulator* (Jupyter)
11. **Generative AI & Prompting** *(How modern LLMs predict tokens & follow clear prompts)* — **Concept:** Role, context & format prompting | **Project:** *Smart Science Quiz Maker* (Browser)
12. **AI Safety & Ethics** *(Testing hallucinations, dataset bias & digital privacy)* — **Concept:** Unfair training data & balanced datasets | **Project:** *Biased vs. Fair Model Challenge* (Teachable Machine)
