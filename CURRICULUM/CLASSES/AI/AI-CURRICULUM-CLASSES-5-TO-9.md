| # | Content & Core Concept *(Taught in ALL Grades 5–9)* | Class 5 | Class 6 | Class 7 | Class 8 | Class 9 |

| **1** | **What Is AI?**<br>*(Fixed rules vs. learning from examples)* | **Project:** *AI or Not? Mystery Box* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Sort familiar examples into AI and ordinary automation.<br>**Learn:** Recognize fixed rules, learned patterns and AI limitations. | **Project:** *Beat the Rule-Bot* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Play against a fixed-rule bot and a bot that learns from examples.<br>**Learn:** Compare programmed rules with learning. | **Project:** *Animal Guesser* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare a rule-based guesser with an example-based classifier.<br>**Learn:** Identify clues, patterns and prediction errors. | **Project:** *Rules vs. Smart Spam Filter* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare keyword rules with a small example-based message classifier.<br>**Learn:** Explain why learned predictions differ from fixed rules. | **Project:** *Game Bot Comparator* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare a fixed opponent with one that adapts to past moves.<br>**Learn:** Distinguish rule-based behavior, learning and feedback. |
| **2** | **AI in Everyday Life**<br>*(Recognition, recommendations, predictions and safe use)* | **Project:** *Smart School Game* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Match school problems to useful AI tools.<br>**Learn:** Recognize AI uses, mistakes and privacy risks. | **Project:** *Waste Sorting Demo* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Explore how an example-based sorter can group waste pictures.<br>**Learn:** Understand image recognition and why people check results. | **Project:** *Movie Recommender* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Explore suggestions based on fictional viewing preferences.<br>**Learn:** Understand recommendations, preferences and limitations. | **Project:** *Smart Message Detector* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Test a prepared detector on fictional messages.<br>**Learn:** Understand classification, false alarms and safe data use. | **Project:** *School Notes Finder* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare keyword search with a prepared similarity-based search demo.<br>**Learn:** Understand search, relevant results and checking sources. |
| **3** | **Computational Thinking**<br>*(Patterns, logical decisions and step-by-step problem solving)* | **Project:** *Robot Direction Puzzle* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Guide a robot using a sequence of arrows.<br>**Learn:** Practice sequencing, planning and fixing mistakes. | **Project:** *Treasure Maze* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Plan and test a route through a maze.<br>**Learn:** Break problems into steps and debug instructions. | **Project:** *Number Detective* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Find a hidden number by choosing useful questions.<br>**Learn:** Practice logical decisions and efficient searching. | **Project:** *Escape-Room Planner* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Arrange clues and decisions into a working plan.<br>**Learn:** Practice decomposition, conditions and testing. | **Project:** *Shortest Route Finder* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare routes on a small grid using a guided visual activity.<br>**Learn:** Understand systematic search and comparing solutions. |
| **4** | **Python Programming Basics**<br>*(Output, input, variables, conditions and loops at suitable depth)* | **Project:** *Magic Quiz* (Python (guided))<br>**Why:** Change questions and answers in a short starter program.<br>**Learn:** Use print, input, variables and simple if/else; explore a guided loop. | **Project:** *Rock-Paper-Scissors* (Python)<br>**Why:** Build a game against a random computer choice.<br>**Learn:** Use variables, conditions, loops and random choices. | **Project:** *Math Battle Game* (Python)<br>**Why:** Build a quiz that repeats and tracks points.<br>**Learn:** Use loops, conditions, scores and simple functions. | **Project:** *Text Adventure* (Python)<br>**Why:** Create a story where choices change the outcome.<br>**Learn:** Use functions, conditions, loops and debugging. | **Project:** *Score & Habit Tracker* (Python)<br>**Why:** Record fictional scores or habits and calculate summaries.<br>**Learn:** Use functions, loops and basic input validation. |
| **5** | **Understanding Data**<br>*(Collect, organize, label and clean information)* | **Project:** *Superhero Team Maker* (Python (guided))<br>**Why:** Store and choose heroes from a simple list.<br>**Learn:** Understand lists, items and selecting data. | **Project:** *Cricket Card Lookup* (Python)<br>**Why:** Store fictional player cards and look up details.<br>**Learn:** Use lists, dictionaries and key-value pairs. | **Project:** *Cricket Data Analyzer* (Python)<br>**Why:** Compare fictional cricket scores and find totals and averages.<br>**Learn:** Use lists, dictionaries, simple calculations and clean data. | **Project:** *School Canteen Analyzer* (Python)<br>**Why:** Organize fictional sales and compare popular snacks.<br>**Learn:** Use tables represented by lists and dictionaries, totals and data cleaning. | **Project:** *Movie Recommendation Data* (Python)<br>**Why:** Organize fictional movie ratings and rank simple matches.<br>**Learn:** Use structured records, filtering and sorting; distinguish rules from learning. |
| **6** | **How AI Learns**<br>*(Training examples, testing, predictions and improvement)* | **Project:** *Pencil vs. Eraser AI* (Browser (Teachable Machine or prepared classifier))<br>**Why:** Train with object examples and test unseen pictures.<br>**Learn:** Understand labels, training, testing and mistakes. | **Project:** *Fruit Image Classifier* (Browser (Teachable Machine or prepared classifier))<br>**Why:** Train on fruit pictures and test different appearances.<br>**Learn:** Understand varied examples, classification and prediction errors. | **Project:** *Simple Number Predictor* (Python (guided notebook))<br>**Why:** Fit a simple predictor to small example pairs and test new values.<br>**Learn:** Understand learning from data, prediction and test errors. | **Project:** *Spam Classifier* (Python (guided notebook))<br>**Why:** Train a small classifier on fictional labeled messages and test new ones.<br>**Learn:** Understand features, labels, accuracy and false predictions. | **Project:** *Train-and-Test AI Predictor* (Python (guided notebook))<br>**Why:** Train a small model on a prepared dataset, test unseen examples and improve it.<br>**Learn:** Understand train/test separation, accuracy and model limitations. |
