# Class 9 AI & Python Curriculum

## Term 1: The Connected Foundations (Contents 1–6)

| # | Content & Core Concept *(Taught in ALL Grades 5–9)* | Class 9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **What Is AI?**<br>*(Fixed rules vs. learning from examples)* | **Project:** *Game Bot Comparator* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare a fixed opponent with one that adapts to past moves.<br>**Learn:** Distinguish rule-based behavior, learning and feedback. |
| **2** | **AI in Everyday Life**<br>*(Recognition, recommendations, predictions and safe use)* | **Project:** *School Notes Finder* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare keyword search with a prepared similarity-based search demo.<br>**Learn:** Understand search, relevant results and checking sources. |
| **3** | **Computational Thinking**<br>*(Patterns, logical decisions and step-by-step problem solving)* | **Project:** *Shortest Route Finder* (Browser)<br>**Why:** Compare routes on a small grid using a guided visual activity.<br>**Learn:** Understand systematic search and comparing solutions. |
| **4** | **Python Programming Basics**<br>*(Output, input, variables, conditions and loops at suitable depth)* | **Project:** *Score & Habit Tracker* (Python)<br>**Why:** Record fictional scores or habits and calculate summaries.<br>**Learn:** Use functions, loops and basic input validation. |
| **5** | **Understanding Data**<br>*(Collect, organize, label and clean information)* | **Project:** *Movie Recommendation Data* (Python)<br>**Why:** Organize fictional movie ratings and rank simple matches.<br>**Learn:** Use structured records, filtering and sorting; distinguish rules from learning. |
| **6** | **How AI Learns**<br>*(Training examples, testing, predictions and improvement)* | **Project:** *Train-and-Test AI Predictor* (Python (guided notebook))<br>**Why:** Train a small model on a prepared dataset, test unseen examples and improve it.<br>**Learn:** Understand train/test separation, accuracy and model limitations. |
