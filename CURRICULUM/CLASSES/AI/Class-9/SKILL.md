# Class 9 AI & Python Curriculum Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-ai-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## Term 1: The Connected Foundations (Contents 1–6)
1. **What Is AI?** *(Fixed Rules vs. Learning from Examples)* — **Concept:** Rule systems, Classical ML & Modern AI | **Project:** *Smart Game Opponent Comparator* (Jupyter)
2. **AI Use Cases** *(How AI Solves Real-World Problems)* — **Concept:** Real-world AI apps in science & daily life | **Project:** *Personal Exam-Prep Assistant* (Colab)
3. **Computational Thinking** *(Decomposition, Patterns & Step-by-Step Logic)* — **Concept:** Algorithmic logic & shortest-path thinking | **Project:** *Grid Escape Path-Finder* (Jupyter)
4. **How AI Works** *(Data + Training/Testing + CPU vs. GPU Speed)* — **Concept:** Data tensors & cloud GPU acceleration | **Project:** *CPU vs. Colab GPU Matrix Race* (Colab)
5. **Programming Basics (Python)** *(ALL taught in every class: `print`, `input`, variables, `if/else`, `loops`)* — **Concept:** Full basics + clean functions & error guards | **Project:** *Smart Score & Habit Tracker CLI* (VS Code)
6. **Essential Data Structures** *(Lists & Dictionaries—how Python organizes AI data)* — **Concept:** Structured tables, arrays & vector lists | **Project:** *Fast Song & Movie Similarity Table* (Colab)

## Term 2: Building Real AI Systems (Contents 7–12)
7. **Machine Learning** *(Training a computer to predict & classify from data)* — **Concept:** End-to-end ML training & evaluation pipeline | **Project:** *Smart Air-Quality & Weather Predictor* (Jupyter)
8. **Computer Vision** *(How computers see pixels, faces, hands & objects)* — **Concept:** Custom object detection + web app UI | **Project:** *Drag-and-Drop Object Finder App* (Colab)
9. **NLP & Chatbots** *(How computers clean, understand & reply to human text)* — **Concept:** Modern NLP pipelines (`Hugging Face`) | **Project:** *Auto-Summarizer & Q&A Bot* (Colab)
10. **Deep Learning** *(How multi-layer neural networks spot complex patterns)* — **Concept:** Transfer learning with vision models (`PyTorch`) | **Project:** *Plant Leaf Health Doctor App* (Colab)
11. **Generative AI & Prompting** *(How modern LLMs predict tokens & follow clear prompts)* — **Concept:** Simple RAG (connecting AI to school notes) | **Project:** *School Handbook AI Assistant* (Colab)
12. **AI Safety & Ethics** *(Testing hallucinations, dataset bias & digital privacy)* — **Concept:** Auditing model accuracy, bias & failure cases | **Project:** *Final AI System Safety Report Card* (Jupyter)
