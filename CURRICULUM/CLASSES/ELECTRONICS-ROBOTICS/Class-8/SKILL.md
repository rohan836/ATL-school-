## 1. Curriculum & Integrated Projects (`Class-8/CLASS-8-CURRICULUM.md`)
- **Term 1 (`June–October`, `Weeks 01–14`):** `SMPS`, DMM Power Audit & Core Basics (`AC vs DC`, `Resistor`, `Breadboard & LEDs`, `Switches`, `Potentiometer & LDR`, `Capacitor`, `Diode`, `Transistor`), Bare ICs (`LM358` + Mic, `LM393`, Bare `5V SPDT Relay`, `IRLZ44N`/`IRF540N` MOSFET, `74HC595` Shift Register, Bare `ULN2003A` & `L293D`), `Arduino Mega 2560`, 8-IR Tracking Array, `0.96"`/`0.91"` `OLED SSD1306`, `MPR121` 12-Ch Touch, Water Flow Sensor, 4/8-Ch Optocoupler Relays, `L298P` Shield, `PCA9685` 16-Ch PWM.
