# Electronics & Robotics Curriculum (Classes 5–9)

**Pure Electronics, Embedded Systems, IoT, PCB, 3D Fabrication & Robotics · `Term 1` (June–October · Weeks 01–14) & `Term 2` (November–March · Weeks 15–28)**

Every grade (Classes 5–9) starts in **June (Week 01)** with lab safety, power, `AC vs DC`, multimeter checks, and essential components (`Resistor`, `Breadboard`, `LED`, `Switches`, `Potentiometer`, `LDR`, `Capacitor`, `Diode`, `Transistor`) before advancing across a balanced 28-week year where every component row maps directly to **5 hands-on projects (`Project 1`–`Project 5`)** inside the same curriculum table.

> **Pure Electronics Only:** AI topics are taught separately under `CURRICULUM/CLASSES/AI/`.

## Safety

Students build circuits using low-voltage DC power (`3V–14.8V`), never probe mains `230V` outlets directly, use LiPo charging bags, and wear safety goggles at soldering, cutting, rotary, and drilling benches.
