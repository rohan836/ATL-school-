# Electronics & Robotics Curriculum

**Atal Thinking Lab | One shared curriculum for Classes 5–9**

This is one common eight-module curriculum for all Classes 5–9, not a separate curriculum for each class. Teachers adjust depth and project complexity for students' age and experience.

| Module | Topics and practical work |
|---|---|
| **Module 1: Discovering Electricity** | What is electricity?; atoms, electrons, and electric charge; conductors and insulators; open and closed circuits; batteries, wires, switches, and loads. **Practical:** Build a simple LED circuit. |
| **Module 2: Voltage, Current & Resistance** | Voltage: the electrical push; current: the flow of charge; resistance: opposition to current; AC and DC; series and parallel circuits; Ohm's Law: V = I × R; resistor color codes. **Practical:** Measure voltage, current, and resistance. |
| **Module 3: Electronic Components** | Resistors; LEDs and diodes; capacitors; potentiometers; LDRs and thermistors; push buttons and switches. **Practical:** Component identification and testing. |
| **Module 4: Transistors & Switching** | What is a transistor?; NPN and PNP transistors; base, collector, and emitter; transistor as an electronic switch; transistor as an amplifier; relay working principle. **Projects:** LED switch, touch switch, relay control. |
| **Module 5: Sensors & Output Devices** | What is a sensor?; LDR, IR, ultrasonic, and temperature sensors; buzzers and speakers; DC motors; servo motors; motor drivers. **Projects:** Night lamp, alarm, distance indicator. |
| **Module 6: Introduction to Microcontrollers** | What is a microcontroller?; Arduino / ESP32 introduction; digital input and output; analog input; PWM and brightness control; reading sensors; programming basics. **Projects:** Traffic light, sensor alarm, servo control. |
| **Module 7: Robotics Foundations** | What is a robot?; robot inputs, processing, and outputs; wheels, chassis, and mechanisms; DC motor control; servo-based mechanisms; obstacle detection. **Projects:** Mini robot, obstacle-avoiding robot. |
| **Module 8: Design & Innovation** | Identify a real-world problem; plan a circuit; select components; build a prototype; test and troubleshoot; improve the design. **Final project:** Exhibition. |

## Lab Safety

- Student-built circuits use low-voltage DC. Students must not probe household mains or build exposed mains-powered circuits.
- Teach AC concepts with diagrams or teacher-prepared, isolated low-voltage demonstrations.
- Use current-limiting resistors. Do not drive motors or high-current servos directly from microcontroller GPIO pins; use suitable drivers and power supplies.
- Verify board/module pinouts, logic levels, and component ratings; supervise tools and soldering.
