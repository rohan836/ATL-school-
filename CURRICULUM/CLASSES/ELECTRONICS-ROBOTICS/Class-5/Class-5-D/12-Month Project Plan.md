# Class 5 — 12-Month Electronics & Robotics Project Plan

This plan assigns one project per month. Batch C and Batch D follow the same project sequence, but their weekly sessions are conducted separately.

| Month | Project Name | Main Learning Concept |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Interactive LED Traffic Signal | LEDs, resistors, and sequential circuits |
| Month 2 | Automatic Night Lamp | LDR light sensor and automatic switching |
| Month 3 | First-Press Quiz Buzzer | Pushbuttons, inputs, and outputs |
| Month 4 | Pushbutton Electronic Dice | Digital logic and random numbers |
| Month 5 | Touch-Controlled LED | Touch sensing and transistor switching |
| Month 6 | Water-Level Indicator | Water conductivity and sensing |
| Month 7 | Reaction-Time Challenge | Timing, inputs, and outputs |
| Month 8 | Clap-Activated Light | Sound sensing and switching |
| Month 9 | Obstacle-Avoiding Robot | Ultrasonic sensor and motor control |
| Month 10 | Line-Following Car | IR sensors and robot navigation |
| Month 11 | Smart Parking System | Distance sensing and automated indicators |
| Month 12 | Mini Smart Home Model | Integrated sensors, outputs, and automation |
