# Class 5 Electronics & Robotics Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-electronics-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## 1. Curriculum & Integrated Projects (`Class-5/CLASS-5-CURRICULUM.md`)
- **Term 1 (`June–October`, `Weeks 01–14`):** Safety PPE, 9V/USB power, closed circuits, conductors/insulators, AC vs DC, DMM continuity, Resistors, Breadboard, LEDs, switches, Series & Parallel, Potentiometer, LDR, Capacitors, Diodes, Transistors, Active Buzzers, Piezo disc, RGB LED, Haptic Motor, Jumper Kit, `Arduino Uno R3`, 5V 2A adapter.
- **Term 2 (`November–March`, `Weeks 15–28`):** BO Gear Motors, `SG90` Micro Servo & tester, `HC-SR04` Ultrasonic, `IR` obstacle, `SW-520D` Tilt switch, `HC-05` Bluetooth, 1-Ch Relay, `ISD1820` Voice playback, Modular construction kit, cardboard/craft chassis tools, Complete Robotics & Automobile Mechanism DIY kits.
- **Boundary:** All 5 hands-on projects for every row live directly inside `Class-5/CLASS-5-CURRICULUM.md` (`Project 1`–`Project 5`). Never create a separate `PROJECTS.md` or `TEACHER-NOTES.md`. No student soldering, no bare ICs, no AI topics.
