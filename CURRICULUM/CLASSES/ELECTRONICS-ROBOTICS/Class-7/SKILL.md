# Class 7 Electronics & Robotics Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-electronics-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## 1. Curriculum & Integrated Projects (`Class-7/CLASS-7-CURRICULUM.md`)
- **Term 1 (`June–October`, `Weeks 01–14`):** Lab safety, AC vs DC & Pen DMM (4000-count), Core Passives/LEDs/Switches/Semiconductors, `Zener` diodes, Wire Strippers/Nipper/Ferrule Crimper, PPE Soldering Station/Zero-PCB/Desoldering/Heat-Shrink, `Arduino Uno R3` C++, `16×2 I2C` & bare `HD44780` LCD, 4-Digit `TM1637` & bare 7-Segment, `8×8 MAX7219` Matrix, `4×4 Keypad`, `FSR` & Flex sensors, `LM35` Temp & Pulse Heart-Rate sensor.
- **Term 2 (`November–March`, `Weeks 15–28`):** `L293D` Shield & `TB6612FNG`, `ULN2003` & `28BYJ-48` Stepper, 12V High-Torque Gear Motors, `MG995/996R` & `360°` Servos, `433/315 MHz RF`, `RC522 RFID`, `DS3231 RTC` & EEPROM, `SD` SPI reader, `TCS3200` Color sensor, Digital Vernier Caliper, `FDM 3D Printer` + UPS, hacksaws/files, cordless/electric drivers & drill, Bio-Medical STEM Capstone.
- **Boundary:** All 5 hands-on projects for every row live directly inside `Class-7/CLASS-7-CURRICULUM.md` (`Project 1`–`Project 5`). Never create a separate `PROJECTS.md` or `TEACHER-NOTES.md`. No 2-layer PCBs, no ESP8266/Raspberry Pi, no AI topics.
