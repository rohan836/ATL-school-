## Completion requirements

A project may be marked complete when students have:

- [ ] Built or assembled the project.
- [ ] Completed the circuit or system diagram.
- [ ] Tested the project.
- [ ] Identified and addressed at least one problem, where applicable.
- [ ] Explained the working principle.
- [ ] Submitted the individual assignment.
