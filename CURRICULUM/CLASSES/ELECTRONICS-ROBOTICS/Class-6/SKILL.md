# Class 6 Electronics & Robotics Skill

Use with `../AGENTS.md` and `/.agents/skills/build-electronics-lesson/SKILL.md`.

## 1. Curriculum & Integrated Projects (`Class-6/CLASS-6-CURRICULUM.md`)
- **Term 1 (`June–October`, `Weeks 01–14`):** Safety gear, 12V 2A adapter, AC vs DC, Continuity Tester, `DMM`, Resistors, Breadboard, LEDs, switches & DIP arrays, Mini Proto Shield, Potentiometer & `3362P` Trimpot, LDR & `10kΩ` NTC Thermistor, Capacitor/Diode/Transistors, `LM7805/7809/7812` regulators, 10-Segment Bargraph, 1-Digit 7-Segment, `LM555` Astable & Monostable, 2-Ch Relay, `Arduino Nano`, `DHT11`, Soil `FC-28` & Raindrop sensors.
- **Term 2 (`November–March`, `Weeks 15–28`):** Sound/Big Sound mic modules, `TTP223B`/`TTP22X`/`KY-036` touch sensors, Laser diode, `TSOP1738` IR pair, `HC-SR501` PIR, Dual-Axis Joystick, `MG90S` metal-gear servo, `L293D` H-bridge module, `HM-10 BLE`, Submersible 12V water pump, drawer/pegboard organization, measuring rules & sanding, Agritech & Water/Sanitation STEM kits.
- **Boundary:** All 5 hands-on projects for every row live directly inside `Class-6/CLASS-6-CURRICULUM.md` (`Project 1`–`Project 5`). Never create a separate `PROJECTS.md` or `TEACHER-NOTES.md`. No student soldering, no bare op-amps, no AI topics.
