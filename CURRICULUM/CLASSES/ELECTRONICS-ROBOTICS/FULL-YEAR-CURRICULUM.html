Annual Electronics & Robotics Curriculum (Classes 6–10)

Structured 32-Week Progression: Resistors & Circuits → AC/DC & Power → Transistors & ICs → Microcontrollers → Smart Sensors → Wireless IoT & Robotics

Week Unit / Topic Core Concepts Hands-on Lab Build Hardware Used
Stage 1: Basic Passive Electronics & Resistors (Weeks 1–6)
01 Electricity Fundamentals & Lab Safety Atomic structure, charge, Voltage (V), Current (I), Resistance (R), Ohm's Law (V = I × R), lab safety protocols. Use a digital multimeter: measure cell voltages, continuity test jumper wires, and calculate basic V-I-R values. Digital Multimeter, 9V/AA batteries, test leads.
02 Resistors & Current Limiting Resistor color codes (4-band and 5-band), tolerance ratings, power dissipation, protecting LEDs from overcurrent. Decode color bands, verify values using multimeter; calculate and test current-limiting resistors for standard 5mm LEDs. Assorted Resistors (100Ω–100kΩ), Multimeter, 5mm LEDs.
03 Breadboard Prototyping & Closed Circuits Solderless breadboard internal anatomy, power distribution rails, tie-point rows, closed circuits vs. open circuits vs. short circuits. Assemble a properly wired breadboard circuit with battery power, current-limiting resistor, LED, and polarity check. Breadboard (400 or 800 tie), LEDs, 220Ω resistors, 9V snap/pack.
04 Series & Parallel Circuits Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) in series, Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) in parallel, equivalent resistance (R_total). Build dual-LED series vs. parallel circuits side-by-side; measure voltage drops and compare LED intensities. Breadboard, LEDs, 220Ω/1kΩ resistors, Multimeter, jumpers.
05 Switches, Buttons & Hardware Logic Mechanical switches (SPST, SPDT, push buttons), Normally Open (NO) vs. Normally Closed (NC), contact bouncing. Construct hardware logic circuits: 2 switches in series (AND gate behaviour) and 2 switches in parallel (OR gate behaviour). Tactile push buttons, slide switches, LEDs, breadboard.
06 Capacitors & RC Timing Dielectrics, capacitance (C = Q/V), electrolytic vs. ceramic capacitors, charging/discharging curves, RC time constant (τ = R × C). RC soft-off timer: wire an LED circuit that gradually dims and turns off over several seconds after releasing the push button. Capacitors (10µF, 100µF, 470µF), 10kΩ resistors, push buttons.
Stage 2: AC vs. DC, Diodes & Power Conversion (Weeks 7–10)
07 AC vs. DC Fundamentals Alternating Current (AC) vs. Direct Current (DC), sinusoidal waves, frequency (Hz), peak vs. RMS voltage, mains safety. Observe DC battery polarity vs. AC signal generation (low-voltage AC adapter / signal source), measure RMS voltage with DMM. Digital Multimeter, DC battery packs, low-voltage AC source (6V-12V).
08 Semiconductor Diodes & Rectification P-N junction physics, forward bias (0.7V threshold), reverse bias, breakdown voltage, standard diodes (1N4007) vs. Zener/Schottky. Test diode one-way conductivity; wire a reverse-polarity protection circuit for sensitive electronics. 1N4007 Diodes, 1N4148 Diodes, LEDs, Multimeter, breadboard.
09 AC to DC Power Supply & Filtering Half-wave rectification, full-wave Bridge Rectifier, smoothing ripple with filter capacitors, DC linear regulation (7805 IC). Build a bridge rectifier with 4 diodes and a filter capacitor; regulate pulsating DC to a clean 5V output using an LM7805. 4× 1N4007 diodes (or bridge package), 1000µF capacitor, LM7805 regulator.
10 Safe Soldering & Perfboard Assembly Milestone 1 Solder alloy (60/40), rosin core flux, iron temperature, tinning, cold joints, safety goggles, perfboard component layout. Transfer the 5V regulated DC power supply or dual-LED indicator circuit from breadboard onto permanent dotted perfboard. Soldering station, stand, solder wire, perfboard, safety goggles.
Stage 3: Transistors, Timers & Active Components (Weeks 11–14)
11 BJT Transistors as Electronic Switches Bipolar Junction Transistors (NPN vs. PNP), Base-Collector-Emitter pins, base current control, saturation and cut-off regions. Build a transistor switch: use an NPN BC547 to drive an audible buzzer or high-power LED from a weak finger-touch signal. BC547 / 2N2222 Transistors, 5V Buzzer, 1kΩ/10kΩ resistors.
12 Variable Resistors & Light Sensing Potentiometers as variable voltage dividers; Light Dependent Resistors (LDR) photo-conduction, voltage divider equation. Automatic dark-detecting night lamp: wire an LDR and transistor circuit where an LED turns ON automatically as room lights turn OFF. LDR, 10kΩ Potentiometer, BC547 Transistor, LED, breadboard.
13 Relays & Inductive Load Driving Electromagnetism, relay coil energization, NO/NC contacts, back-EMF inductive spikes, flyback protective diodes. Build a transistor-driven 5V relay module circuit with a flyback diode to safely switch an isolated auxiliary DC lamp. 5V Relay Module, 1N4007 Diode, BC547, DC lamp / motor.
14 Integrated Circuits: The 555 Timer Internal block diagram of NE555 (comparators, flip-flop, discharge transistor), Astable multivibrator mode, duty cycle. Build an adjustable dual-LED flasher (police strobe) using an NE555 timer and potentiometer without any programming. NE555 Timer IC, capacitors, resistors, 10kΩ pot, dual LEDs.
Stage 4: Programmable Microcontrollers (Weeks 15–20)
15 Microcontroller Architecture & IDE Microprocessor vs. Microcontroller, ATmega328P architecture, flash memory, SRAM, GPIO pins, USB-Serial bridge, Arduino IDE. "Hello World": connect Arduino board, configure COM port, write and upload custom blink routines with modified timing. Arduino Uno or Nano, USB cable, PC with Arduino IDE.
16 Digital Outputs & Sequencing pinMode(), digitalWrite(), delay(), microcontroller execution cycle, multi-pin coordination without electrical shorting. Build a 4-way Traffic Light intersection with Red, Yellow, Green cycles and a synchronized pedestrian crossing signal. Arduino Uno, Red/Yellow/Green LEDs, 220Ω resistors, breadboard.
17 Digital Inputs & Pull-Up Logic digitalRead(), floating input states, pull-up vs. pull-down resistors, internal INPUT_PULLUP register, mechanical switch debounce. Push-button latch toggle: press once to turn lamp ON, press again to turn OFF (software state tracking). Push buttons, 10kΩ resistors, LED indicators, Arduino.
18 Analog Outputs & PWM Pulse Width Modulation (PWM), duty cycle, analogWrite(), 8-bit resolution (0–255), tone() and noTone() audio synthesis. Build a breathing LED mood lamp; program a piezo buzzer to play custom melodies and alarm siren frequencies. PWM pins (~3, ~5, ~6, ~9), Piezo buzzer, LEDs, breadboard.
19 Analog Inputs & ADC Conversion 10-bit Analog-to-Digital Converter (0–1023), reference voltage (5V/1024 ≈ 4.9mV), analogRead(), map() math scaling. Rotary dimmer & light meter: read potentiometer and LDR values via analog pins; map them to adjust LED brightness smoothly. 10kΩ Potentiometer, LDR, Analog pins (A0–A5), LEDs.
20 Actuators: Servomotors & DC Motor Drivers Milestone 2 Servo PWM pulse timing (50Hz), Servo library, H-bridge motor driver theory (L298N/L293D), motor back-EMF and external battery power. Position an SG90 micro-servo by angle knob; control DC motor forward, reverse, and speed regulation via L298N driver board. SG90 Servo, L298N Driver, BO Motor, external battery pack.
Stage 5: Smart Sensors, Displays & Data Telemetry (Weeks 21–25)
21 Serial Communication & Telemetry UART protocol, TX/RX registers, baud rates (9600 vs. 115200), Serial Monitor debugging, Serial Plotter waveform graphing. Stream live sensor readings to PC terminal; send bidirectional ASCII commands ('1'=ON, '0'=OFF) to trigger lab hardware. Arduino Uno, PC Serial Monitor / Plotter, sensors.
22 Acoustic Distance Sensing (HC-SR04) Piezoelectric acoustic transceivers, speed of sound in air (343 m/s), time-of-flight equation (d = v × t / 2), pulseIn() timing. Ultrasonic Parking Radar: accurately measure distance to obstacle; sound faster buzzer beeps as distance narrows. HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, buzzer, LED bar / indicator.
23 Alphanumeric Displays (16×2 I²C LCD) Character LCD matrix, I²C two-wire bus (SDA & SCL), hex slave addresses (0x27 / 0x3F), reducing 16 pins down to 4 wires. Visual Sensor Console: display live distance readings, custom greeting banners, and animated status messages on a 16×2 LCD screen. 16×2 LCD with I²C backpack module, HC-SR04 sensor, Arduino.
24 Environmental & Motion Sensors DHT11 single-bus digital protocol (temperature & relative humidity), HC-SR501 PIR pyroelectric infrared motion sensing, Fresnel lens. Build a Classroom Security & Climate Monitor: read temperature/humidity and trigger alert countdown upon motion detection. DHT11 sensor, HC-SR501 PIR sensor, 16×2 LCD, buzzer.
25 Infrared Line Sensors & Robotics Drivetrain Milestone 3 TCRT5000 IR reflectance sensors, black absorption vs. white reflection, differential drive kinematics, caster wheel balance. Assemble 2WD mobile robot chassis; calibrate dual IR tracking sensors to track a black electrical tape line course autonomously. 2WD chassis kit, 2× BO motors, dual IR line sensors, L298N driver.
Stage 6: Wireless IoT, Cloud Sensors & Capstone (Weeks 26–32)
26 Introduction to IoT & ESP Microcontrollers ESP8266 NodeMCU / ESP32 architecture, 3.3V logic level constraints, Wi-Fi Station (STA) mode vs. Access Point (SoftAP) mode. Connect ESP board to lab Wi-Fi network; scan available SSIDs, obtain assigned DHCP IP address, and print to Serial Monitor. ESP8266 / ESP32 Dev Board, micro-USB cable, PC.
27 Local Web Server & Appliance Control HTTP request-response cycle, embedded HTML/CSS web server in microcontroller flash, parsing query parameters for pin actuation. Smartphone-controlled home automation: host a web dashboard on ESP; toggle 5V relay module and LEDs from any mobile phone browser. ESP32 / ESP8266, 5V single-channel relay module, smartphone browser.
28 Bluetooth Telemetry & Remote Control Bluetooth Serial Port Profile (SPP) vs. BLE, UART bridging, AT commands, mobile terminal pairing. Build a Bluetooth-controlled remote smart lamp: transmit control commands from an Android Bluetooth serial terminal app. HC-05 module or onboard ESP32 Bluetooth, smartphone app.
29 IoT Cloud Dashboards & Telemetry Cloud IoT architectures, REST API endpoints, publishing sensor telemetry over HTTP/MQTT to cloud services (Blynk / ThingSpeak). Cloud Weather Station: log live temperature and humidity data to a cloud dashboard; view real-time graphical charts remotely. ESP32/ESP8266, DHT11 sensor, ThingSpeak / Blynk cloud account.
30 Autonomous Navigation: Obstacle Avoidance Acoustic radar scanning, servo pan mounting, decision tree: stop → sweep sensor left/right → calculate clearest path → steer motors. Mount ultrasonic sensor on a front SG90 servo; program autonomous robot to navigate a maze without colliding with obstacles. 2WD mobile robot, HC-SR04 sensor, SG90 servo, mounting bracket.
31 Capstone Engineering: Design & Prototyping Engineering lifecycle: problem statement, block diagrams, schematic capture, breadboard prototyping, modular code integration. Teams begin chosen capstone build: (1) Smart Automated Home, (2) Autonomous Rover with Telemetry, or (3) IoT Greenhouse. Full ATL component pool, chassis, sensors, breadboards.
32 Capstone Showcase & Technical Review Final Capstone Technical presentation, system block diagrams, user manuals, failure analysis, real-world constraints and future scope. Grand Showcase: demonstrate completed projects to teachers, peers, and visitors; submit technical documentation and logbooks. Completed team projects, project logbooks, presentation posters.
