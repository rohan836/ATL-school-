Lab Safety

  • Student-built circuits use low-voltage DC. Students must not probe household mains or build exposed mains-powered circuits.
  • Teach AC concepts with diagrams or teacher-prepared, isolated low-voltage demonstrations. Keep relay projects on the low-voltage side.
  • Use current-limiting resistors and check component polarity before powering a circuit.
  • Do not drive motors or high-current servos directly from microcontroller GPIO pins. Use suitable drivers and power supplies.
  • Verify board/module pinouts, logic levels, and current/voltage limits; supervise tools and soldering.
