# AI Safety Guidelines — Classes 5–9

- No face recognition, student photographs, or identifiable student data in classroom AI activities.
- Do not upload student personal information to external AI tools.
- Verify AI-generated claims and citations; outputs can be incorrect or fabricated.
- Discuss fairness, privacy, consent, representation, and potential harm age-appropriately.
- Treat generated media as potentially synthetic, not verified evidence.
- Gas sensors are teaching components, not life-safety alarms.
- Never drive a servo, motor, pump, or relay coil directly from a GPIO pin.
- Mains-powered builds are out of scope for student projects.
